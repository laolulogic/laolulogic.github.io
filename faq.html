Straight answers about how I work, what things cost, how long they take and what you own at the end. Can't find yours? Ask me on WhatsApp.

Working together

What does a CRM & AI automation specialist do?

I set up and clean up CRMs such as Follow Up Boss, Lofty, GoHighLevel and HubSpot, connect them to your website, forms, ads and calendars with Zapier, Make or n8n, and add AI agents that answer calls and messages, qualify leads and book appointments. Every lead gets a fast reply and a clear next step, without manual work.

How much does a project cost?

Every project is scoped individually. After a free 30-minute call you receive a clear proposal with a fixed scope and a fixed price, so there are no hourly surprises. See the packages for what's typically included.

How long does a project take?

Focused projects such as a CRM setup or a set of Zapier automations usually take a few days to two weeks. Migrations, AI voice agents and multi-workflow systems typically take two to four weeks.

How do we get started?

Book a free 30-minute strategy call, message me on WhatsApp or send the contact form. I'll map what to automate first and send a clear proposal with a fixed scope.

Can you work in my time zone?

Yes. I work with clients worldwide and keep a flexible schedule, so calls and updates fit your working hours.

Can I hire you through Fiverr or Upwork?

Yes. I'm a Level 2 seller on Fiverr and also available on Upwork, with the same quality and the platform's payment protection.

CRM & automation

Which CRMs do you work with?

Follow Up Boss, Lofty (Chime), GoHighLevel, HubSpot, BoldTrail (kvCORE), Zoho CRM, Airtable and REsimpli, plus most tools that offer an API or a Zapier or Make integration. The full list is on the services page.

Zapier, Make or n8n: which will you use?

The cheapest tool that reliably does the job. Zapier is quickest for simple, well-supported apps; Make handles complex branching at lower cost; n8n suits AI agents and high volumes, especially self-hosted. I explain the trade-offs in this guide.

Can you fix an automation someone else built?

Yes. I regularly diagnose broken Zapier zaps, failed webhooks and messy CRM routing, fix them and add monitoring so problems are caught early.

Do you offer ongoing CRM management?

Yes. Monthly management covers lead routing, campaigns, tags, pipelines and integrations, with small improvements every month. It's also available white-label for agencies.

Real estate CRM

Which real estate CRMs do you set up?

Follow Up Boss, Lofty (formerly Chime), BoldTrail (kvCORE) and GoHighLevel are my core specialisms. I also work in Sierra Interactive, CINC, BoomTown, Real Geeks, Wise Agent, LionDesk, Propertybase, HubSpot, REsimpli and REI Reply, and connect Zillow, Realtor.com, Meta Lead Ads, IDX websites, dialers and mortgage systems like ARIVE.

Can you migrate my leads without duplicates?

Yes. I clean and map the data first, run a dry-run import on a small sample, then import in batches with matching rules, including custom ID fields when leads have no email or phone.

Can you handle A2P 10DLC texting registration?

Yes. I handle brand and campaign registration and have taken clients from rejection to full approval for their text marketing.

I'm busy. Can you just manage it for me?

That's what monthly management is for: I keep an eye on routing, plans, tags and integrations so your pipeline keeps moving while you're out showing homes.

AI agents

What can an AI voice agent do for my business?

It answers every call, day or night, captures the caller's details, answers common questions, books appointments into your live calendar and writes a summary to your CRM. It hands off politely when a human is needed.

Will an AI agent make things up?

Not if it's built properly. I write prompts with strict guardrails: the agent never invents prices, timelines or policies, only uses the information you approve and escalates anything it isn't sure about.

Data & ownership

Is my data safe?

I work inside your own accounts with the access you grant, coordinate two-factor logins with you and never share customer data outside the project.

Who owns what you build?

You do. Every account, workflow, prompt and asset lives in your accounts, with a recorded walkthrough and written notes so your team can run it without me.
