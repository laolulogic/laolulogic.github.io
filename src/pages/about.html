01 My story

I build the systems behind businesses that never miss a lead.

I'm a CRM expert and AI automation specialist, and the founder of Laolu Logic. I work with real estate agents, teams and brokerages, marketing agencies, coaches and service businesses that are great at what they do but are losing leads to messy systems and manual work.

I started inside Lofty and Follow Up Boss, building smart plans, routing rules and pipelines for real estate teams. The more I built, the more I saw the same pattern: the CRM was never the whole problem. The leads lived in five different apps and nobody owned the follow-up. So I learned to connect everything with Zapier, Make and n8n, and then to add AI voice agents that pick up the phone when nobody else can.

Today I design the whole journey, from the first form fill to the booked appointment. I keep it simple, test it properly and hand it over with training, so you never depend on guesswork.
