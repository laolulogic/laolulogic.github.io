01 My story

I build the systems behind businesses that never miss a lead.

Most of the businesses I meet aren't short of leads. They're short of follow-up. The leads sit in five different apps, the CRM is half set up, and the person who was "going to fix it" moved on months ago.

I started inside real estate CRMs: Lofty and Follow Up Boss, building smart plans, routing rules and pipelines for agents and teams. Project after project, the pattern was the same. The CRM was never the whole problem. So I learned to connect everything around it with Zapier, Make and n8n, then added AI agents that reply, qualify and book when nobody else can.

Today I design the whole journey, from the first form fill to the booked appointment and the deal that closes. I keep it simple, test it with real data and hand it over with training videos, so your team owns the system instead of depending on me.
