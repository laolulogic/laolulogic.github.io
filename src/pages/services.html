FAQ

Before you ask.

01Which CRMs do you work with?

Follow Up Boss, Lofty (Chime), GoHighLevel, HubSpot, BoldTrail (kvCORE), Zoho CRM and REsimpli, plus most tools that offer an API or a Zapier or Make integration.

02How long does a project take?

Focused projects like a CRM setup or a set of Zapier automations usually take a few days to a week. Migrations, AI voice agents and multi-workflow systems typically take two to four weeks. You get a clear timeline before we start.

03How much does it cost?

Every project is scoped individually. After a short call I send a clear proposal with a fixed scope and price, so you know exactly what you're paying for.

04Is my data safe with you?

Yes. I work inside your own accounts with the access you grant, coordinate two-factor logins with you and never share customer data outside the project. You own every account, workflow and asset I build.

05Can you fix something someone else built?

Absolutely. A lot of my work is diagnosing broken zaps, failed webhooks and messy CRM routing, then fixing them and adding alerts so problems don't go unnoticed again.

06Do I need to buy new software?

Usually not. I start with the tools you already pay for and only recommend something new when it clearly saves money or time.
