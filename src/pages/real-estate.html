Real estate FAQ

Agents ask me.

01Which real estate CRMs do you set up?

Follow Up Boss, Lofty (formerly Chime), BoldTrail (kvCORE) and GoHighLevel are my core specialisms. I also work in Sierra Interactive, CINC, BoomTown, Real Geeks, Wise Agent, LionDesk, Propertybase, HubSpot, REsimpli and REI Reply, and connect Zillow, Homes.com, Realtor.com, Meta Lead Ads, IDX websites, dialers and mortgage systems like ARIVE.

02Can you migrate my leads without duplicates?

Yes. I clean and map the data first, run a dry-run import on a small sample, then import in batches with matching rules, including custom ID fields when leads have no email or phone.

03Can you handle A2P 10DLC texting registration?

Yes. I handle brand and campaign registration and have taken clients from rejection to full approval for their text marketing.

04I'm busy. Can you just manage it for me?

That's what monthly management is for: I keep an eye on routing, plans, tags and integrations so your pipeline keeps moving while you're out showing homes.
