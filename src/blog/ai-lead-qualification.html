Your best salespeople shouldn't spend their day asking "what's your budget?" and "when are you looking to move?". AI can ask those questions instantly, around the clock, and hand your team leads that are ready to talk.

Three ways AI qualifies leads

  1. Conversational: an AI agent replies by text, chat or voice, asks a few questions and books qualified leads straight into the calendar.
  2. Scoring: AI reviews the information you have and scores each lead against your criteria.
  3. Research: AI looks up a company or prospect and adds context before a human calls.

Conversational qualification

An AI agent asks two or three questions, for example timeline, budget and what they need, then books the call or routes the lead. In GoHighLevel, the AI Employee can do this inside your existing workflows (see the AI Employee guide). On the phone, an AI voice agent does the same.

AI scoring with reasons

For a wholesale distributor with a large list of retailers, I built a system that researches each company and scores it 0 to 100 on five weighted criteria, with the reasoning, the product category to pitch and a suggested sales approach (read the case study). The reasoning matters: sales teams trust scores they can understand.

Keep humans in the loop

  • AI scores are advisory; they never auto-reject a lead.
  • Agents use guardrails and never invent prices or promises.
  • Anything unusual is escalated to a person.

Connect it to your CRM

Every answer, score and summary should land in the CRM: custom fields for the answers, a score field, a tag for the tier and a note with the reasoning. Then your existing routing rules and smart lists put the hottest leads first.
