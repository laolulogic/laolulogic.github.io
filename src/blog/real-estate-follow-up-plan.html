Online real estate leads rarely buy this week. Many are months away. The agent who wins them usually isn't the best salesperson; it's the one who stayed in touch without being annoying. Here's the plan I build into CRMs for agents and teams.

Phase 1: the first 48 hours

  • Minute 1: automated text and email referencing what they asked about.
  • Minute 5: call task for the agent (see speed to lead).
  • Hour 4: second call attempt.
  • Day 2: text with something useful, like matching listings or a quick market note.

Phase 3: months 2 to 12

Switch to a slower rhythm: one useful email every two to four weeks, a text every month or two and a quarterly call task. Content that works: new listings in their search, market updates, home-buying or selling guides and local news.

Phase 4: past clients

Closed clients are your best referral source. Give them their own plan: closing anniversary, home-value check-ins and holiday touches. For a Florida team, I imported 70 past clients with closing dates so anniversary gifts and messages run automatically.

Rules that keep it human

  • Pause automation the moment a lead replies.
  • Write in your own voice. Short beats clever.
  • Only email and text people who gave permission, and honor opt-outs instantly.
