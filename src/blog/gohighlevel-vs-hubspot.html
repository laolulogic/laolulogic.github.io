I'm a HighLevel Certified Admin and hold three HubSpot Academy certifications, so I get this question a lot. Both are excellent. They're built for different kinds of selling.

At a glance

GoHighLevelHubSpot
Core strengthTexting, calls, funnels, bookingEmail marketing, content, reporting
Best forLocal services, coaches, agencies, real estateB2B, SaaS, content-led sales teams
AutomationPowerful workflows and AI EmployeePolished workflows, sequences and scoring
Ease of setupNeeds a careful buildEasier out of the box
AgenciesSub-accounts and white-labelPartner program

Choose GoHighLevel if…

  • Most of your leads want a fast text or call back.
  • You need booking, reminders and missed-call text-back.
  • You want funnels, forms and CRM in one bill.
  • You're an agency managing several clients.

Start with my GoHighLevel setup checklist.

Choose HubSpot if…

  • Your sales cycle is longer and email-driven.
  • You rely on content, SEO and newsletters.
  • You want strong reporting and lead scoring out of the box.
  • Several teams (marketing, sales, service) share one CRM.

HubSpot gets even better with automation around it. I've built n8n workflows that score and enrich every new HubSpot contact, alert reps to hot activity and archive stale records (read the case study).

The real deciding factor

The best CRM is the one your team will use every day. Pick the tool that matches how your customers want to be contacted, then set it up properly. A well-built mid-range CRM beats a premium one nobody updates.
