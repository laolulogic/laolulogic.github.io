For a plumber, a roofer, a clinic or a real estate agent, a missed call is often a missed job. Callers rarely leave voicemails anymore; they just call the next business on Google. Missed-call text-back is the simplest automation I build, and one of the most profitable.

How it works

  1. A call comes in and nobody answers.
  2. Within seconds, the caller gets a text from your business number.
  3. If they reply, the conversation continues by text and your team is notified.
  4. If they don't, a follow-up text goes out later, then the lead is handed to a person.

The message that gets replies

Hi, this is Sam from Bright Roofing. Sorry we missed your call! We're on a job right now. What can we help you with? We'll get right back to you.

Use your name and business, apologize briefly, ask an open question and promise a reply. Avoid links in the first message; they look like spam.

Make it smarter

  • Reply detection: split positive replies (route to the team) from negative ones like "wrong number" (stop the sequence).
  • Separate Google Business Profile calls: calls from your listing can get their own message and tracking.
  • Business-hours logic: a different message at night that offers a booking link.
  • Opt-out handling: "STOP" sets Do Not Disturb automatically.
