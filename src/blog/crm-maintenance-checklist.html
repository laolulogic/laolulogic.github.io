A CRM isn't a one-time project. Every month brings new duplicates, a changed integration, leads nobody touched and team members who joined or left. Thirty minutes of maintenance a month keeps it trustworthy. Here's the checklist I run for clients on monthly management.

1. Lead flow

  • Are leads still arriving from every source? Compare with last month.
  • Is routing assigning leads to the right people?
  • How many new leads had no contact within your target time?

2. Stale and stuck leads

  • Leads with no activity in 30+ days: move to nurture or a pond.
  • Deals stuck in one stage too long: review with the owner.

3. Automations and integrations

  • Check error logs in Zapier, Make, n8n and the CRM.
  • Confirm key automations produced the expected results, not just "success".
  • Reconnect any expired app connections.

Why this matters: automations fail quietly.

4. Data quality

  • Merge new duplicates.
  • Fix missing sources, owners and stages.
  • Remove tags created outside your agreed list.

Need a deeper reset first? See CRM data cleanup.

5. Messaging health

  • Email bounce, unsubscribe and complaint rates.
  • SMS opt-out rate and any carrier filtering.
  • Sequences still relevant? Update dates, offers and listings.

6. Reporting

Leads by source, contact rate, appointments booked and deals won. Share a one-page summary with the team so everyone sees what's working.

7. Users and access

  • Remove anyone who has left.
  • Check roles and permissions for new team members.
  • Confirm two-factor authentication is on.
