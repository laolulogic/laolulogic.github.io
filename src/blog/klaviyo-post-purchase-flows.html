For most e-commerce brands, acquiring a customer costs more than their first order earns. The profit is in the second and third orders. Post-purchase flows in Klaviyo are how you earn them, automatically.

The six flows to run

  1. Thank-you (instant): confirm the order and set expectations.
  2. Delivery and usage (on delivery): tips to get the most from the product.
  3. Review request (a few days after delivery): social proof for future buyers.
  4. Cross-sell (1 to 3 weeks): products that pair with what they bought.
  5. Replenishment: reminders timed to when the product runs out.
  6. Win-back: for customers who haven't reordered in your usual window.

Split first-time and repeat buyers

A conditional split changes everything. First-time buyers need reassurance and education. Repeat buyers need recognition and relevant offers. In one Klaviyo build, the flow sends an instant thank-you, timed follow-ups and a conditional split that tags repeat customers automatically for future segmentation (read the case study).

Timing tips

  • Time usage and review emails from delivery, not purchase.
  • Base replenishment timing on real reorder data.
  • Don't stack flows: exclude people already in a more relevant flow.

Add SMS carefully

Texts work well for shipping updates and time-sensitive offers, with consent and opt-out handling built in (see SMS follow-up best practices).
