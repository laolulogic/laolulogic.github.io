Privacy policy.

Plain English: what I collect, why, and what you can ask me to do with it. Last updated November 2026.

Who I am

This website is run by Ibrahim "Laolu" Awosanya, trading as Laolu Logic. If you have any question about your information, email laolulogic@gmail.com.

What I collect

  • Contact form: your name, email, and anything else you choose to add (phone or WhatsApp, company, website, current CRM, services, timeline and your message).
  • Booking form: your name, email, optional phone, the time you picked, your time zone and any notes.
  • Free checklist: your first name and email, so I can send you the checklist.
  • Analytics (only if you accept): pages visited, how you arrived, device and browser type, and anonymous interaction data such as clicks and scrolling.

How I use it

  • To reply to you, schedule and confirm calls, and send what you asked for.
  • To prepare a proposal if you ask for one.
  • To understand which pages help visitors, so I can improve the site.

I never sell your information, and I don't share it with anyone except the services that make this website work.

Services that process your information

  • Google Workspace (Apps Script, Sheets, Gmail and Calendar) stores form submissions, sends confirmation emails and creates calendar invites.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and may log technical data such as IP addresses for security.
  • Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity measure how the site is used, only after you accept analytics.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) handles any conversation you start through a WhatsApp link.

Cookies

Essential settings, such as your light or dark theme and your cookie choice, are saved in your browser. Analytics cookies are only set if you click "Accept". You can change your choice at any time with the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of every page.

How long I keep it

Enquiries and bookings are kept for as long as needed to work together and for normal business records, then deleted. Analytics data is kept for the shortest period the tools allow.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information I hold about you, or ask me to stop contacting you. Email laolulogic@gmail.com and I'll act on it promptly. Depending on where you live (for example the UK, the EU or California), you may have further rights under local law, including the right to complain to your data protection authority.

Security

Submissions travel over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and are stored in password-protected business accounts that only I can access.

Children

This website is meant for businesses and is not directed at children under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear on this page with a new date.
