Sound familiar?

You pay for leads. Then they go cold.

Three problems I fix in almost every real estate CRM I open.

Speed to lead

Slow first response

Zillow, Realtor.com and Facebook leads wait hours for a reply, and the agent who called first wins. Online leads often contact several agents at once.

Fix: an instant text and email (or AI call) within seconds, a timed call task for the agent, and an escalation if nobody claims the lead.
Ownership

Nobody owns the follow-up

Leads sit unassigned, tagged five different ways, with no plan after day two. Most buyers aren't ready for months, so the follow-up has to outlast them.

Fix: round robin or rules-based routing, ponds for unclaimed leads, and smart plans that keep every lead moving for 12 months.
Hidden revenue

A database full of missed deals

Thousands of old leads and past clients nobody has contacted in a year, mixed with duplicates, bad numbers and people who opted out.

Fix: cleanup, Do Not Contact checks and a staged reactivation that starts new conversations without hurting deliverability.
