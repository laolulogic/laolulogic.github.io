The challenge

Every new client was onboarded differently, and Meta blocked direct Messenger access without a developer app and review.

What I built

  1. A four-step onboarding SOP: get data, clean (dedupe, DNC scrub, segment), upload to GoHighLevel, tag and launch
  2. Smart lists and reactivation workflows in each GoHighLevel sub-account
  3. An AI receptionist demo and an n8n drip-campaign prototype for sales calls
  4. A Make mailhook that turns Marketplace email alerts into Follow Up Boss contacts and notes, with no Meta developer tokens
