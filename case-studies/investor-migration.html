The challenge

Thousands of seller leads, a live Zapier feed from REsimpli and appointments that had to land on the correct agent every time, inside a shared brokerage account where mistakes affect everyone.

What I built

  1. Lead imports validated with dry runs before production
  2. A tag inventory and cleanup plan approved by the client before any change
  3. 17 routing rules, including per-agent appointment handoffs by tag
  4. Fixed role overlaps so each person holds exactly one seat
  5. Diagnosed a frozen Zapier plan and replayed 37 held leads safely
  6. Smart plans for nurture, post-closing and anniversary touches
