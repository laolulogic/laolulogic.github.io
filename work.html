Case study · Lofty CRM · Real estate

Reviving 3,287 dormant leads while the agent was away

A US agent preparing to leave for months of training had a large, untouched database and no one to work it. He needed it organized, compliant and quietly generating conversations without him.

The challenge

Years of leads with no structure: no clear stages, no segmentation and no safe way to email thousands of contacts without damaging the sending domain.

What I built

  • Sorted every lead into clear pipeline stages and verified contact data
  • A five-email reactivation smart plan with 3 to 5 day spacing and a "No Response" tag at the end
  • A staged rollout, starting with 151 leads, to protect sender reputation
  • Unsubscribed and Do Not Contact records excluded; 145 unreachable profiles filtered out
  • Routing that sends financing leads to the lender and hot or ready leads straight to the agent
  • A recorded walkthrough so the client could audit everything from his phone

The result

Clean execution from day one: 100% delivery with zero bounces and a 42.86% open rate on the first touch. The client approved with a five-star review and asked for ongoing monthly management.
