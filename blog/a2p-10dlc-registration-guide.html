You built a perfect follow-up sequence, but your texts aren't arriving. If you're texting US contacts from a local number, the most likely reason is A2P 10DLC. Here's what it is and how to get approved the first time.

What A2P 10DLC is

US mobile carriers require businesses that text from standard 10-digit numbers to register who they are and what they're sending. Registration has two parts:

  • Brand registration: your legal business details.
  • Campaign registration: the type of messages you send, how people opt in and sample messages.

Platforms such as Twilio, GoHighLevel and most real estate CRMs with texting submit this for you, but you have to provide the right information.

What to prepare before applying

  1. Exact legal business name, address and tax ID matching official records.
  2. A live website with a privacy policy stating you don't share mobile numbers for marketing.
  3. SMS terms: message frequency, "Msg and data rates may apply", how to opt out (STOP) and get help (HELP).
  4. Forms with a clear consent checkbox or statement for text messages.
  5. Two or three sample messages that match your use case and include your business name.

Frequently asked questions

What does A2P 10DLC stand for?

Application-to-Person messaging over 10-digit long codes, meaning business texts sent from standard local US phone numbers.

How long does A2P 10DLC approval take?

Brand registration is often quick; campaign review can take from a few days to a couple of weeks, longer if it is rejected and resubmitted.

Why was my A2P campaign rejected?

The most common reasons are a website without a privacy policy or SMS terms, missing opt-in language on forms, sample messages that do not match the use case and inconsistent business details.
