Key takeaways

  • Follow Up Boss is the best pure CRM for teams that live on calls, texts and accountability.
  • Lofty is an all-in-one: CRM plus IDX website, marketing and lead generation.
  • Choose Follow Up Boss if you already have a website and lead sources you like.
  • Choose Lofty if you want one platform for website, leads and follow-up.

I build in both Lofty and Follow Up Boss for agents, teams and brokerages. They're both excellent, but they solve different problems. Picking the wrong one usually means paying for features nobody uses, or bolting on tools the other one would have included.

Follow Up Boss: built for follow-up

Follow Up Boss does one thing brilliantly: it makes sure agents follow up. Smart lists become a daily call list, action plans automate texts and emails, ponds catch unworked leads and the reporting shows exactly who is responding. It connects to almost every lead source, dialer and website platform.

Best for: teams and brokerages with their own website and lead sources who want speed and accountability. My Follow Up Boss setup checklist covers how I build it.

Lofty: the all-in-one platform

Lofty combines a CRM with IDX websites, lead generation, marketing and AI assistant features. Smart plans handle follow-up, routing rules assign leads, and the website feeds leads straight into the CRM with property activity attached.

Best for: agents and teams who want one platform for website, leads and follow-up. In my Lofty reactivation case study, smart plans re-engaged a 3,287-lead database with a 42.86% first-touch open rate.

Questions to decide

  1. Do you already have a website and lead sources you like? Lean Follow Up Boss.
  2. Do you want one bill and one login for everything? Lean Lofty.
  3. How many agents, and how tech-savvy? Larger, mixed-skill teams often adopt Follow Up Boss faster.
  4. Do you depend on specific integrations? Check them before you switch.

Switching later is possible

You're not locked in. I've moved teams between CRMs, including an investor team from REsimpli to Lofty with 17 routing rules (read the case study). The key is a clean migration plan; see how to migrate a real estate CRM without losing leads.

Frequently asked questions

Is Lofty the same as Chime?

Yes. Chime rebranded as Lofty. It is the same platform, with CRM, IDX websites, marketing and AI features.

Can I migrate from Lofty to Follow Up Boss, or the other way?

Yes. I clean and map the data, run a test import and move contacts, notes and tags in batches without duplicates.

Which is easier for agents to learn?

Most agents find Follow Up Boss faster to learn because it is focused on follow-up. Lofty has more features, which means more to learn.
