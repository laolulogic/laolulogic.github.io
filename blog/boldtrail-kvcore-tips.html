Key takeaways

  • Your brokerage may provide BoldTrail; most agents use a fraction of it.
  • Check lead source tracking and routing before anything else.
  • Smart campaigns only work if leads are tagged consistently.
  • A 20-minute weekly routine keeps the pipeline moving.

Many brokerages provide BoldTrail (formerly kvCORE) to their agents, and many agents use only a small part of it. A few setup changes usually make a big difference.

1. Check lead sources and routing

  • Make sure every lead source is tracked, so you know which ones produce deals.
  • Confirm routing rules: who receives each type of lead and how fast they're notified.
  • Set expectations for response time and a fallback if leads aren't claimed (see speed to lead).

2. Tag consistently

Smart campaigns and filters depend on tags. Agree a short list for timeline, buyer or seller, area and price range, and use them the same way every time. Clean up duplicates before building anything new (see CRM data cleanup).

3. Use smart campaigns for long-term follow-up

Set up campaigns for new buyer leads, new seller leads, long-term nurture and past clients. Write the messages in your voice and keep them short. My 12-month follow-up plan gives the timing.

4. Make the daily list obvious

Use filters or hot sheets so each morning starts with new leads, then active conversations, then follow-ups due. If agents have to search for who to call, they won't call.

5. Connect what's missing

If leads arrive from tools BoldTrail doesn't integrate with directly, an automation can bridge the gap. Always "find before create" so integrations don't duplicate contacts.

6. A 20-minute weekly routine

  1. Review new leads with no contact.
  2. Check campaign replies and hand-raisers.
  3. Update stages on active deals.
  4. Clear out junk and duplicates.

Frequently asked questions

Is BoldTrail the same as kvCORE?

Yes. kvCORE is now part of BoldTrail, Inside Real Estate's platform. Many agents still call it kvCORE.

Can BoldTrail connect to other tools?

Yes, through its integrations and through automation tools where available. I check what your brokerage's setup allows before recommending changes.

Should I leave BoldTrail for Follow Up Boss or Lofty?

Not necessarily. If your brokerage provides BoldTrail, a proper setup often solves the problem. If you need features it lacks, a migration may make sense.
