Vapi vs Retell AI: choosing a platform for your AI receptionist

Key takeaways

  • Both platforms can run a natural-sounding receptionist that books appointments.
  • Vapi offers more control over models, voices and transcribers.
  • Retell AI is quicker to get running with strong conversation handling.
  • The calendar and CRM integration matters more than the platform choice.

Vapi and Retell AI are two of the most popular platforms for building AI voice agents. I've built receptionists on both, for a roofing contractor and a medical practice among others. Here's how they compare in practice.

What both do well

  • Natural voices with fast, sub-second responses.
  • Inbound and outbound calls on your own number.
  • Tools and webhooks for booking appointments and updating your CRM.
  • Call recordings, transcripts and summaries.

Vapi: maximum control

Vapi lets you choose each layer: the transcriber, the language model and the voice. That means you can tune cost and quality precisely. My Vapi receptionist used Deepgram, GPT-4o mini and an ElevenLabs voice for about $0.11 per minute and roughly one second of latency (see the case study).

Choose Vapi if you want fine-grained control or plan to scale call volume and optimize cost.

Retell AI: quick to production

Retell AI focuses on getting a reliable, natural conversation running quickly, with strong handling of interruptions and turn-taking. For one client, a Retell agent books appointments through an n8n workflow that looks up the right calendar, checks for conflicts and either books or offers another time.

Choose Retell AI if you want a smooth receptionist live fast with less configuration.

What matters more than the platform

  1. The prompt: your real FAQs, tone and strict guardrails.
  2. The booking logic: live availability and conflict checks.
  3. The CRM handoff: contact created, summary logged, team alerted.
  4. Ongoing tuning: reviewing real calls weekly at first.

My recommendation

For most small businesses, either platform works. I choose based on call volume, integrations and how much tuning the client wants. Read AI voice agents for small business for the bigger picture, or see the AI Front Desk package.

Frequently asked questions

Which is cheaper, Vapi or Retell AI?

Costs depend on the voice, model and telephony you choose on each platform. In practice both run at cents per minute; my Vapi receptionist build ran at about 11 cents per minute.

Can both book appointments into my calendar?

Yes, through tools or webhooks. I usually connect them to n8n or the CRM so the agent checks availability, avoids conflicts and books the slot.

Can I switch platforms later?

Yes. The prompt, knowledge and booking workflow can be moved; only the platform configuration needs rebuilding.
